PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: June 2026 | GDPR, CCPA, POPIA Compliant

1. WHO WE ARE

SG.IO Global operates a multi-tenant SaaS platform available in 150+ countries. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data under global regulations.

2. GLOBAL REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

GDPR (EU/EEA Users)

If you are in EU/EEA, your rights include: access, correction, deletion, portability, and restriction. Data transfers comply with Article 44. Our Data Protection Officer: dpo@sg.io

CCPA (California Users)

California residents have right to: know, delete, opt-out of sale, and non-discrimination. We do not sell personal information.

POPIA (South Africa/African Users)

Personal information is protected under POPIA. Information Regulator contact available. Cross-border transfers comply with regulations.

Other Regions

We comply with: UK Data Protection Act, Australia Privacy Act, Canada PIPEDA, Singapore PDPA, Japan APPI, India DPDP Act, Brazil LGPD, and local laws.

3. WHAT DATA WE COLLECT

Account Information

  • Name, email, phone number
  • Business name, address
  • Payment method (encrypted)
  • Password (bcrypt hashed)

Business Data

  • Products, prices, inventory
  • Customer names, emails, orders
  • Transaction histories
  • Analytics and usage data

Technical Data

  • IP address, browser type
  • Device information
  • Login times, access logs
  • Cookies (with your consent)

4. HOW WE USE DATA

We use data to:

  • Provide and improve our service
  • Process payments and billing
  • Send service notifications
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Detect and prevent fraud
  • Improve security
  • Generate analytics (aggregated)

We do NOT: Sell data, use for marketing without consent, share with unrelated third parties.

5. DATA RESIDENCY CHOICES

You can choose where your data lives:

  • Default (Auto-optimized): USA (AWS Virginia), EU (AWS Frankfurt), or Asia (AWS Singapore)
  • EU Only (GDPR): Frankfurt, Germany - No USA storage
  • Custom (Elite): On-premises, private cloud, hybrid

6. DATA ENCRYPTION & SECURITY

  • At Rest: AES-256 encryption
  • In Transit: TLS 1.3 (HTTPS)
  • Passwords: Bcrypt + salt (never plain text)
  • Database: Encrypted fields for sensitive data
  • Backups: Encrypted copies, daily backups
  • Monitoring: 24/7 security monitoring

7. YOUR RIGHTS

Right to Access

Download all your data in CSV/JSON format. Response: 30 days.

Right to Correction

Update your personal information anytime via your dashboard.

Right to Deletion

Request account deletion. Data erased within 30 days (legal holds excepted).

Right to Portability

Export all data in machine-readable format for transfer elsewhere.

Right to Opt-Out

Opt-out of marketing emails, tracking, analytics anytime.

Right to Lodge Complaint

Report to your local regulator (ICO, CNIL, OAIC, etc). We cooperate fully.

8. DATA BREACH NOTIFICATION

If a breach occurs:

  • GDPR users: Notified within 72 hours
  • Other users: Notified within 30 days
  • Regulators informed
  • Details provided: What, when, who affected, what we're doing

9. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

We use these services (with Data Processing Agreements):

  • Cloud: Amazon AWS (data processing)
  • Payment: Stripe, PayPal, Flutterwave (payment processing)
  • Email: SendGrid (transactional emails)
  • Analytics: Internal only (no Google Analytics)

We never share data with unrelated companies or advertisers.

10. COOKIES & TRACKING

We use cookies for:

  • Authentication (login sessions)
  • User preferences (language, timezone)
  • Security (CSRF protection)

Cookies are essential for functionality. You can disable, but some features won't work. We do NOT use tracking cookies or pixels.

11. DATA RETENTION

  • Account Active: Data kept as long as needed
  • Account Deleted: Erased within 30 days
  • Logs: Kept 90 days (for security)
  • Backups: Retained per legal requirements

12. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

SG.IO is for businesses (18+). We don't intentionally collect data from children. If we discover this, we delete immediately.

13. POLICY CHANGES

We may update this policy anytime. Material changes will be emailed to you. Continued use = acceptance.

IMPORTANT: As a multi-tenant platform, your data is isolated from other businesses. We use database-level tenant scoping to ensure complete separation.
