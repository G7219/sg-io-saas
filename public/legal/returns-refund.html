5. CANCELLATION VS REFUND

Want to Cancel (After 14 days)?

  • Go to Settings → Subscription
  • Click "Cancel Subscription"
  • No refund after 30 days, but service continues until payment due date
  • Your data remains accessible

Want a Refund (Within 30 days)?

  • Email billing@sg.io with reason
  • If within 14 days: Full refund
  • If within 30 days: Pro-rated refund
  • After 30 days: No refund available

6. PRO-RATED REFUNDS (Days 15-30)

Formula: (Remaining Days / Days in Month) × Amount Paid

Example:

You purchased Lite plan on June 1st for $30. On June 20th, you request refund.

  • Remaining days: 11 (June 20-30)
  • Days in month: 30
  • Refund: (11 / 30) × $30 = $11
  • You keep: $19 for 19 days of service
